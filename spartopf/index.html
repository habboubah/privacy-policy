Privacy Policy

Ahmad Habboubah built Spartopf, a household budget book, as a free app. This SERVICE is provided at no cost and is intended for use as is.

This page explains how Spartopf handles information, since your privacy matters.

Summary

Spartopf does not collect, transmit, or store any of your data on any server. There is no backend and no account system. Everything you enter — months, income and expenses with their amounts, notes and due days, categories, and settings — is saved only in your device's local app storage and never leaves your device.

Information Collection and Use

The app does not request, collect, or transmit any personally identifiable information. I (the developer) have no access to your data, no server it is sent to, and no way to view, recover, or delete it on your behalf — it exists only on your device.

Third-Party Services

Spartopf does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs, makes no network requests, and does not use cookies. Your device's operating system (iOS) and the App Store may collect standard installation and diagnostic information under their own privacy policies, which are outside this app's control:

  • Apple Privacy Policy

Backups

If you use "Export backup" or "Export as CSV for Excel", the file goes wherever you choose to save or send it (for example iCloud Drive or AirDrop). If your device's own backup (e.g. iCloud Backup) is enabled, your locally stored app data may be included in it. Both are controlled entirely by you — no data is ever sent to the developer, because there is no server.

Data Deletion

You can delete all data at any time in the app (Settings → Delete all data) or by uninstalling the app. There is nothing stored elsewhere to delete.

Children's Privacy

This app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13, because it does not collect information from anyone at all.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if the app's data practices change. Any changes will be posted on this page.

This policy is effective as of 2026-11-01.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or need support with Spartopf, contact me at ahmad.habboubah@gmail.com.
