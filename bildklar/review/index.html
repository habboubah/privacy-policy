Bildklar – sample photos

96 public-domain (CC0) photos and videos with dates and places, for trying Bildklar on a test iPhone: a family's photos at home, a trip to Paris (May 2025), bursts of near-identical shots, an exact duplicate, a saved-again copy, shaky and accidental shots, screenshots, receipts and memes. Credits are in credits.csv inside the zip.

Download the zip (55 MB)

  1. On the iPhone, tap the button above in Safari and download the zip.
  2. Open it in the Files app (Downloads): tapping the zip unzips it.
  3. Open the folder bildklar-sample-photos › photos, tap Select, then Select All.
  4. Tap Share, then Save 96 Items (they go to Photos with their dates and places).
  5. Open Bildklar.
