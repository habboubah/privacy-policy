Privacy Policy

Ahmad Habboubah built “Bildklar AI: Photo Cleaner”, an app that organizes the photos on your iPhone or Android phone: it finds your trips, near-identical photos and blurry photos. This page explains how the app handles information.

Summary

Bildklar works entirely on your device. There is no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising. Your photos, where and when they were taken, and every decision you make in the app stay on your device. The developer receives nothing and has no way to see any of it.

Your Photos

Bildklar asks for access to your photo library, because that is what it organizes. You can give it access to all photos or only to the ones you choose (“Limited Access” on the iPhone, “Select photos” on Android 14 and newer), and change this at any time in your device's settings.

With that access, the app reads for each photo: the date it was taken, its location (if the photo has one), its size and dimensions, whether it is a favourite or a screenshot, and a small preview. From the preview it computes a few numbers on the device — how sharp the photo is, its brightness and contrast, and a short “fingerprint” used to find near-identical photos. On the iPhone, when you open a group of near-identical photos, it also checks with Apple's on-device Vision framework whether faces are in focus and eyes are open. An image model built into the app (SigLIP 2) also turns each preview into a short list of numbers, which lets you search by what is in a photo (“dog”, “receipt”) and find similar photos. The model runs only on your device, works without internet, and is never trained on your photos. None of this leaves your device.

Place names (“Paris, May 2026”) come from a list of places built into the app. The app does not look up locations online.

Photos that are stored only in iCloud are not downloaded by the app; it uses the preview your iPhone already has.

What the App Stores on Your Device

  • A scan cache with the information above (the image model's numbers too) and small preview images, so the app does not have to read your whole library again each time. It is rebuilt from your photos whenever needed and is not included in device backups.
  • Your decisions: renamed, merged, split and hidden trips, the best photos you picked, the photos you chose to keep, and the albums or folders you created. If your device's own backup (iCloud Backup or Google's backup) is on, this small file may be included in it. You can erase it in the app (Settings → Start over).
  • Display settings such as language and light or dark mode.

Changes to Your Photo Library

Bildklar never changes, deletes or moves a photo by itself, and never changes a photo's contents. Only when you ask:

  • Albums (iPhone): the app creates an album in your Photos app with the photos of a trip.
  • Folders (Android): after Android's own confirmation, the photos of a trip are moved into a folder (Pictures/Bildklar/…).
  • Deleting: after your confirmation and the system's, photos go to Recently Deleted (iPhone) or the trash (Android), where you can restore them for 30 days.
  • Smaller copies: after your confirmation, the app saves a smaller copy of a video or photo, or a still photo from a Live Photo, next to the original, with the original's date and place. The original stays until you delete it yourself.

Purchases

For now everything in Bildklar is free and nothing is for sale. Later, unlocking some features may become a one-time purchase through the App Store or Google Play. The payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google; the app only asks the store whether the unlock has been bought and remembers the answer on your device. The developer receives no payment details. Google's Play Billing Library, which Android apps use for purchases, may send Google its own technical information about the purchase connection under Google's privacy policy.

Third-Party Services

The app contains no analytics, tracking, advertising or crash-reporting libraries, and no Firebase. Your device's operating system and the store you got the app from may collect standard installation, purchase and diagnostic information under their own privacy policies, which are outside this app's control:

  • Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

Feedback and Links

“Send feedback” opens a new mail in your own mail app, with the app's version and your system's version (for example “Bildklar 1.0 · iOS 26.1”) filled in. Nothing is sent unless you send that mail yourself. “Privacy” and “Rate Bildklar” open your browser or the store; the app itself fetches nothing.

Data Deletion

Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored. Your photos and the albums or folders you created stay in your library. There is nothing stored elsewhere to delete.

Children's Privacy

The app is meant for adults. It does not collect information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all.

Place Names
