Privacy Policy

Ahmad Habboubah built The Simple Shopping List, a shopping list app, as a free app. This SERVICE is provided at no cost and is intended for use as is.

This page explains how the app handles information, since your privacy matters.

Summary

The app does not collect, transmit, or store any of your data on any server. There is no backend and no account system. Everything you enter — lists, items, quantities, notes, photos, list types and suggestions, and settings — is saved only in your device's local app storage and never leaves your device.

Information Collection and Use

The app does not request, collect, or transmit any personally identifiable information. I (the developer) have no access to your data, no server it is sent to, and no way to view, recover, or delete it on your behalf — it exists only on your device.

Camera and Photos

The app asks for access to the camera or photo library only when you choose to add a photo to a list or an item. The picture is shrunk and stored on your device only. It is never uploaded anywhere.

Third-Party Services

The app does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs, makes no network requests, and does not use cookies. Your device's operating system (iOS or Android) and the store you got the app from (the App Store or Google Play) may collect standard installation and diagnostic information under their own privacy policies, which are outside this app's control:

  • Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

Backups

If you use the app's "Export backup" feature, the file goes wherever you choose to save or send it (for example iCloud Drive, Google Drive or AirDrop). If your device's own backup (iCloud Backup on an iPhone, Google's backup on an Android phone) is enabled, your locally stored app data may be included in it. Both are controlled entirely by you — no data is ever sent to the developer, because there is no server.

Sharing a List

If you tap "Share list", the app puts that one list (its name, items, quantities and notes — no photos) into a link, which you send yourself through the app you choose, such as Messages or WhatsApp. The list is stored inside the part of the link after the "#", which browsers never send to any server: the page the link opens (on GitHub Pages) reads it on the receiver's phone only. Nothing is sent to the developer, and nothing is shared unless you send it.

On Android

The Android app is the same app. It contains no Google services, no Firebase and no advertising or analytics library. It asks for no permission beyond what the system grants every app; the camera or photo picker opens only when you choose to add a photo.

Feedback and Links

"Send feedback" opens a new mail in your own mail app, with the app's version and your system's version (for example "The Simple Shopping List 1.0.1 · iOS 18.1") filled in. Nothing is sent unless you send that mail yourself. "Rate the app" and "Privacy policy" open the store or your browser; the app itself fetches nothing.

Data Deletion

You can delete all data at any time in the app (Settings → Reset app) or by uninstalling the app. There is nothing stored elsewhere to delete.

Children's Privacy

This app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13, because it does not collect information from anyone at all.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if the app's data practices change. Any changes will be posted on this page.

This policy is effective as of 2026-11-13.
