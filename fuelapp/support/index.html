Tankenblick Support

Questions, a problem or an idea? Write to me at ahmad.habboubah@gmail.com. Please add your iPhone or Android model, the system version and the app version (Menu → About this app).

I see no stations

Tankenblick shows fuel prices in Germany only. Allow your location "While Using the App", or tap "Choose a place" and enter a German town or postcode (for example 10115 Berlin). Closed stations are marked "Closed"; the "Open now only" switch above the list hides them.

Where do the prices come from?

From the Markttransparenzstelle für Kraftstoffe (MTS-K) of the German Federal Cartel Office, through tankerkoenig.de (CC BY 4.0). Stations report price changes within minutes; the app refreshes every minute while the list is open.

A price is wrong

Open the station and tap "Report wrong price". You can add the price you saw.

Price alerts or reminders don't arrive

Allow notifications for Tankenblick in the phone's settings. The phone decides when the app may check prices in the background, so an alert can come later than the price change; opening the app checks at once.

Without internet

The app shows the last prices it loaded, with their time.
