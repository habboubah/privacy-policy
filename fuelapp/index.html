Privacy Policy

Ahmad Habboubah built Tankenblick, a fuel price app for Germany, for iPhone and Android. This page explains how Tankenblick handles information. It states the same facts as the privacy page in the app (Settings → Privacy).

Account

The app has no account and no sign-in. Nothing is synced to a cloud.

No tracking

No advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no crash reporting. There is no advertising id.

What goes to our price server

For the search and the details: your location or the place you chose, rounded to about 1 km, with radius and fuel; a station's id when you open it or save it as a favourite; for a favourite with a price alert, the app also loads its prices in the background now and then, with the station ids only (never the alert price); when you send a report, the station id, the problem and an optional corrected price.

Our server code keeps no logs of these requests. The server runs on Cloudflare, which processes the IP address of each request in order to deliver it, under Cloudflare's privacy policy.

What goes to the map provider

For the map the app loads map data from the map provider (OpenFreeMap; MapTiler in a build with a MapTiler key). Only the map area is sent, with the IP address as with any request. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors.

What stays only on your phone

Favourites, settings, a place you chose (name, postcode and a rounded point; searching for a place sends nothing), price history, the opening hours of stations you opened, the fuel log and when the app first ran on this phone (date and version). They never leave the phone. The last prices you saw, kept for offline use, are stored on the phone only as well.

Price alerts stay on the phone too: the alert price, the last-checked time and when an alert fired. The check and the notification run on the phone, and nothing about alerts is sent.

Updates

At start and now and then, the app asks the App Store or Google Play whether a newer version exists. Only the app's id goes to Apple or Google, nothing about you.

Location

The app asks for your location only while you use it ("While Using the App"), never in the background. You can use the app without it by choosing a place instead.

Prices

The prices come from Tankerkönig (MTS-K). Data from tankerkoenig.de, licensed under CC BY 4.0.

Places

The list of places and postcodes for the search is stored in the app. It comes from GeoNames, licensed under CC BY 4.0. The app downloads nothing for it.

Payments

Purchases happen only in a version of the app that offers them; the first version sells nothing. When you buy or restore Pro, an anonymous id and the purchase go to RevenueCat, which processes the payment, and to the App Store or Google Play. None of it goes to our price server. The Pro state is also kept on your phone so it holds offline.

Apple and Google

Your device's operating system and the App Store or Google Play may collect standard installation and diagnostic information under their own privacy policies, which are outside this app's control: Apple Privacy Policy, Google Privacy Policy.

Data deletion

Everything the app keeps is on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes it. There is nothing stored elsewhere to delete.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

I may update this policy, for example if the app's data practices change. Any changes will be posted on this page. This policy is effective as of 2026-11-24 and was last updated on 2026-11-26 (update check; the home-screen widget was removed).
